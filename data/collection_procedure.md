# Collection Procedure

1. Keep AC voltage source at 2.5 Vpp and 2.5 V offset.

2. For no object, collect 6 voltages 100 times at frequencies: 1, 2.5, 5, 7.5, and 10 kHz. 

3. For vertical eraser, collect 6 voltages 400 times at 5 kHz frequency. Record 100 measurements for each quadrant. Move the eraser to random positions within the quadrant, with random rotations, while keeping it vertical, for each of the 100 measurements.

4. Repeat step 3 for a finger and a small balloon (2 cm radius).
