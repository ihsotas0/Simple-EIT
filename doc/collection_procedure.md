# Collection Procedure

1. Keep AC voltage source at 2.5 Vpp and 2.5 V offset.

2. Add a standard volume (100 mL) of tap water to the water container in the
   test rig. Use the same tap water if it needs to be replaced.

3. For no object, collect 6 voltages 100 times for each frequency: 1, 5, 10, 20
   kHz. 

4. For vertical eraser, collect 6 voltages 400 times at 5 kHz frequency. Record
   100 measurements for each quadrant. Move the eraser to random positions
   within the quadrant, with random rotations, while keeping it vertical, for
   each of the 100 measurements. Place a small string from A to D and B to C to
   make sure the eraser stays within the quadrant, and to make sure data is
   collected even at the boundaries of the quardrant effectively.

5. Repeat step 3 for a finger and a small balloon (2 cm radius).

6. Total number of measurements: 1200. At 10 seconds per measurement, this
   procedure will take around 4 hours.
